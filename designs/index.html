What changed in the content, and why

Every candidate drops the stale material and features the work you asked for. Details are in CONTENT-BRIEF.md, the single source of truth all eight were built from.

  • Featured: tether, cleat and Black Diamond Brawl, written with their real technical substance rather than one-liners. Earlier work is a small secondary list.
  • Removed: the "grad student seeking roles starting 2021" framing, the phone number, the .edu address, the skill percentage bars, the quarantine counter, and the 2020 resume PDF link. The new bio is role-agnostic so it won't go stale again.
  • Left out: cue — that repo is private, and it's a concerts app rather than the AI tooling in scope.
  • Contact: GitHub and LinkedIn only. Adding an email back is a one-line change.

Unrelated but worth doing: the old index.html had a live Google Maps API key committed in plaintext. Every candidate drops it, but it has been public in the repo's history for years — rotate it in the Google Cloud console regardless of which design wins.

Once you've picked

Say the number. The winner gets promoted to the repo root as index.html, the abandoned template assets (css/, js/, fonts/, scss/, blog.html, prepros-6.config) get deleted, and this designs/ directory comes out. Mixing pieces across candidates is fine too.
